Legal & About

About Garud

Garud is a digital marketplace that connects talented Indian artisans directly with customers. Our platform celebrates India's rich cultural heritage by helping artisans showcase authentic handmade products while providing customers with a trusted shopping experience.

Garud aims to reduce dependency on intermediaries, promote fair trade, and preserve traditional crafts through technology.

Our Mission

To empower artisans by providing them with a digital platform that enables them to reach customers across India while ensuring transparency, fair pricing, and sustainable growth.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted online marketplace for authentic handmade products and support artisan communities through innovation and responsible commerce.
